FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common questions

Is there a minimum commitment?

Our tiered model is designed to be flexible. The GCC Readiness Assessment is a one-time engagement with no ongoing obligation. The GCC Build Program and Executive Advisory operate on a retainer or project basis, typically 3–6 months minimum, but we tailor the duration to your objectives.

Can we mix engagement models?

Absolutely. Many clients start with a Readiness Assessment, then transition into the Build Program. You can also combine the Build Program with Executive Advisory on specific workstreams. We design the engagement around your needs.

What is the ROI timeline?

Most clients see measurable cost optimization within 6–12 months of launching their GCC. Strategic outcomes — talent quality, innovation capacity, governance maturity — build over 12–24 months. We help set realistic expectations from day one.

Do you work with companies outside India?

Yes. We work with organizations globally — US, UK, Europe, Japan, Australia, Singapore. Our team spans multiple time zones and operates with a global perspective and India-first execution.

What if I need something not listed?

Every engagement is unique. If your needs don\u2019t fit neatly into one of our three models, let us know. We\u2019ll design a custom engagement that aligns with your specific objectives.
