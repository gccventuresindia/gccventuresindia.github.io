Prepare Your Venture for Maximum Value

Whether you're planning an M&A transaction, preparing for investor exit, or restructuring for maximum valuation — structured advisory from the first assessment through the final transition.

OUR PROCESS

Assess → Prepare → Execute → Transition

Every engagement follows a structured progression. We don't guess — we assess, build a roadmap, execute with discipline, and manage the transition to ensure sustainable outcomes.

01
Assess
Comprehensive evaluation of your venture's readiness — financial health, governance structure, market positioning, and competitive moats. We identify where value is trapped and where it's being lost.
02
Prepare
Build the case. Restructure operations, clean up financials, strengthen management depth, and position the venture for maximum buyer or investor interest. Typically 6–18 months of preparation.
03
Execute
Run the process — from buyer identification and outreach through due diligence and negotiation. We manage the commercial and strategic dimensions while you stay focused on running the business.
04
Transition
The deal is only half the story. We ensure a structured handover — knowledge transfer, cultural integration, team retention — so the value created doesn't evaporate post-close.
SERVICES

Five Services. One Outcome: Maximum Value.

Each service is designed to address a specific dimension of exit readiness. Most clients engage us across multiple services — because exit preparation is never about just one thing.

Service 01

Exit Readiness Assessment

A comprehensive diagnostic of your venture's exit readiness across five dimensions: financial, operational, governance, market positioning, and leadership depth. This is your starting point.

  • Financial health analysis and gap identification
  • Management team depth and capability review
  • Market positioning and competitive moat assessment
  • Governance and compliance posture evaluation
  • Timeline estimation and readiness roadmap

Timeline: 4–6 weeks · Deliverable: Exit readiness scorecard and improvement roadmap

Outcome: A clear, data-driven understanding of where your venture stands and a prioritised plan to close the gap between current state and maximum exit value.

Service 02

M&A Strategy & Positioning

Strategic advisory for identifying buyers, structuring the approach, and managing the M&A process from first contact through to close. We bring a network of potential acquirers and a structured methodology.

  • Target buyer identification and outreach strategy
  • Teaser and information memorandum development
  • Buyer qualification and strategic fit analysis
  • Offer evaluation and comparison framework
  • Negotiation strategy and terms optimisation

Timeline: 3–9 months depending on complexity · Full-process advisory

Outcome: A qualified pipeline of serious buyers, structured offers to compare, and a negotiation process designed to maximise your position.

Service 03

Valuation Optimization

Many founders leave 30–50% of their venture's value on the table because of poor preparation and positioning. We identify value drivers, strengthen them, and ensure the market prices your venture correctly.

  • Independent valuation benchmarking and analysis
  • Revenue model optimisation for multiple expansion
  • Cost structure and margin improvement roadmaps
  • Customer concentration and dependency reduction
  • Intellectual property and asset leverage strategies

Timeline: 8–16 weeks · Deliverable: Valuation improvement plan

Outcome: A higher, defensible valuation backed by measurable improvements in your venture's fundamental value drivers.

Service 04

Investor Readiness & Fundraising

For ventures preparing for institutional exit — whether through IPO, strategic sale to a PE fund, or other structured exit. We prepare the organisation to pass due diligence at the highest level.

  • Investor targeting and relationship building
  • Financial modelling and forecasting architecture
  • Investment memorandum and data room preparation
  • Management team presentation and pitch coaching
  • Term sheet evaluation and structuring advice

Timeline: 4–8 months · Full fundraising process support

Outcome: A compelling investment narrative, qualified investor interest, and a funding round that

Service 05

Post-Exit Transition Planning

The transition period after exit is critical — whether you're staying on as an advisor, leaving entirely, or moving to the next venture. We design and manage the transition to protect both your interests and the value of the business you built.

  • Personal transition strategy and timeline planning
  • Knowledge transfer and institutional memory preservation
  • Team retention and cultural integration support
  • Succession planning and interim leadership structuring
  • Post-exit performance monitoring and advisory

Timeline: 6–18 months · Post-close engagement available

Outcome: A smooth, controlled transition that protects the value you've built and sets you up for your next chapter — whether that's advisory, a new venture, or retirement.
