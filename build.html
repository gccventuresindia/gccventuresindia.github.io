Our Expertise

Build Your India GCC — From Strategy to Scale

From initial assessment to operational maturity, we guide you through every step of establishing and scaling your Global Capability Centre in India. Our practitioner-led approach ensures that every decision is informed by real-world experience—not theory.

Our Services

End-to-End GCC Setup

01 Market Entry Strategy

We begin with a thorough assessment of your goals, target markets, competitive landscape, and regulatory considerations to build a data-driven entry strategy tailored to your industry and growth timeline.

What It Includes

Stakeholder workshops, competitive benchmarking, market sizing analysis, India region comparison, and a structured go-to-market roadmap with phased investment projections.

Why It Matters

India presents diverse markets with varying incentives, talent pools, and regulatory environments. A structured entry strategy ensures you pick the right city, sector, and operating model.

Outcome

A validated market-entry plan with clear investment targets, regional recommendations, and an estimated timeline to operational maturity.

03 Site Selection & Infrastructure

We evaluate potential locations based on talent availability, infrastructure quality, government incentives, cost of operations, and quality of life, then guide negotiations with real estate partners.

What It Includes

City shortlisting (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, NCR, Chennai), co-working and campus evaluation, lease negotiation support, and technology infrastructure planning.

Why It Matters

Location drives everything. The right site accelerates growth while the wrong one creates long-term drag.

Outcome

Optimal physical location secured with favorable terms, infrastructure mapped, and a transition plan for seamless occupancy.

02 Regulatory & Legal Structuring

We navigate the complex regulatory landscape for foreign direct investment, foreign entity registration, and compliance frameworks, ensuring your GCC is established on a solid legal foundation.

What It Includes

FIEP and RBI registration support, FEMA compliance advisory, company structure design, SEZ/Free Trade Zone eligibility assessment, and ongoing regulatory monitoring.

Why It Matters

Non-compliance can delay operations, trigger penalties, and damage reputation. Expert legal structuring ensures you meet both central and state requirements from day one.

Outcome

Fully registered entity with all regulatory clearances in place, ready for operational launch.

04 Talent Architecture & Recruitment

We design the organizational structure, define role profiles, and lead the initial hiring wave to get your GCC operational quickly.

What It Includes

Org design workshops, JD creation, salary benchmarking, recruitment pipeline setup, employer branding support, and retention strategy development.

Why It Matters

India's talent market is competitive. A structured approach to hiring and retention prevents the high-cost turnover cycle that undermines early-stage GCCs.

Outcome

Leadership team in place, core recruitment pipeline operational, and a talent retention framework to sustain growth.

05 Governance & Compliance Framework

We build the governance structures, reporting frameworks, and compliance systems that enable your GCC to operate as a strategic arm of the organization while meeting global standards.

What It Includes

Board reporting templates, KPI dashboard design, data governance policy, IP protection framework, cross-cultural operating guidelines, and risk management protocols.

Why It Matters

Without proper governance, GCCs become silos rather than value centers. Strong frameworks ensure alignment with headquarters while enabling local autonomy.

Outcome

A governance operating model that ensures compliance, drives accountability, and positions the GCC as a strategic value center.

Our Process

Four Phases to Operational Excellence

01 Discovery & Assessment 2-4 weeks

Deep-dive into business objectives, operational needs, risk profile, and strategic priorities. We benchmark against peers and identify opportunities.

02 Planning & Structuring 4-6 weeks

Develop the detailed roadmap: regulatory strategy, site shortlist, talent plan, governance design, and investment timeline.

03 Build & Launch 8-16 weeks

Execute registration, site setup, initial hiring, and system integration. Go live with the GCC as an operational entity.

04 Scale & Optimize Ongoing

Gradually expand headcount, deepen capabilities, optimize processes, and elevate the GCC to a strategic value center.
