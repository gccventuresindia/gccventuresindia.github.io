PROGRAMS

Four Programmes, One Objective

Each programme is structured around the specific inflection point your career or organisation faces. The format is bespoke — typically 6–12 months with bi-weekly engagement.

Programme 01

Individual Executive Coaching

One-on-one engagement between practitioner and executive. Focuses on the individual's strategic lens, decision-making under ambiguity, executive presence, and the specific challenges their role demands.

  • Strategic decision-making frameworks for complex, high-stakes scenarios
  • Executive presence and board-level communication
  • Stakeholder alignment across geographies and time zones
  • Personal leadership operating model and habit architecture

Format: Bi-weekly 90-minute sessions · 6–12 month engagement · Confidential

Outcome: Sharpened strategic judgment, elevated executive presence, and a repeatable personal operating model that sustains performance through scaling cycles.

Programme 02

Leadership Team Coaching

Engagement with the entire senior leadership team. Addresses team dynamics, cross-functional alignment, and the collective capability required to execute strategy at scale.

  • Leadership team diagnostic and capability gap mapping
  • Working agreement for cross-functional collaboration
  • Strategic planning cycles that actually execute
  • Conflict navigation and high-trust team architecture

Format: Monthly offsites · Bi-monthly review sessions · 6–9 month engagement

Outcome: A leadership team that operates as a unified strategic unit — fewer silos, faster decisions, and collective ownership of outcomes.

Programme 03

Cross-Cultural Leadership

For leaders managing multi-regional operations, particularly India-based GCCs reporting to global HQs. Addresses the cultural, governance, and communication dimensions that generic coaching misses.

  • Cross-cultural communication frameworks for India–global dynamics
  • Talent management across time zones and regulatory environments
  • Building trust-based relationships with global senior stakeholders
  • Navigating power distance, directness, and decision-making norms

Format: Combination of individual and team sessions · Ongoing · Can run alongside coaching programmes

Outcome: Leaders who navigate cultural complexity without losing strategic clarity — building trust across borders while executing on global objectives.

Programme 04

Succession & Continuity Planning

Structured engagement for founders and CEOs preparing for transition — whether handing over to a successor, selling the business, or stepping into an advisory role. This is where strategy meets human capability at its most critical moment.

  • Successor identification and capability development roadmap
  • Knowledge transfer and institutional memory preservation
  • Personal transition planning — from operator to advisor
  • Board alignment and governance structuring for the transition

Format: Intensive engagement · 6–18 month programme · High-touch advisory
